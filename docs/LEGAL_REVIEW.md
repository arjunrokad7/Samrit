# Public legal pages

The landing page and meal finder display a terms notice and link to `/terms` and `/regulations`. No checkbox, acceptance record or consent gate is implemented. A displayed notice alone does not establish enforceable assent.

The terms are a hackathon draft. Before public launch, the operator and qualified counsel must review liability/release wording, identify the operating entity and contact channel, establish a privacy policy and retention process, and decide how to communicate and record any required agreement. No recipient is required to waive statutory rights to explore the prototype.

The meal finder remains fictional and explicitly labeled; these pages do not add live listings or certify food safety.

Official references checked October 24, 2026:

- [NY Agriculture & Markets Law §71-z](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/AGM/71-Z): qualifying donations to charities/nonprofits for free distribution, inspection and knowledge conditions, retained regulatory authority, gleaning insurance.
- [42 USC §1791, including 2023 amendments](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/pdf/USCODE-2023-title42-chap13A-sec1791.pdf): donation and distribution eligibility, qualified direct donors, gross-negligence and intentional-misconduct exceptions.
- [NYC temporary-event checklist](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/doh/business/food-operators/self-inspection-checklist-temporary-events.page): 140°F hot / 41°F cold holding; scoped to this NYC guidance, not universal cooking or statewide rules.
- [NYS food-service regulations](https://www.health.ny.gov/environmental/indoors/food_safety/regs.htm).
- [donateNYC food donation guidance](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/donate/site/DonateFood/Aboutabout).

Confirm current permit-specific rules with the relevant health department before handling food. Statutory protections are conditional and do not replace food-safety duties.
